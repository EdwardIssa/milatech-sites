Referrals and invites

MILA has a referral program. Every account has a referral code, and links you share — including poll links — may carry your code (shown as “?ref=” at the end of the link). After you vote, MILA may offer to let you invite a friend to vote on that poll; sharing is always your choice. When someone opens one of these links and creates an account, we store a referral record — the inviting account, the new account, the code used, and whether the referral has qualified — along with a per-install device identifier used only to prevent abuse of the program, such as one person claiming their own invites. The new account receives 25 coins when it claims the invite, and the inviter receives 50 coins once the new account qualifies by casting 10 votes within 14 days, up to 20 qualified referrals per month. Referral coins are ordinary MILA coins: they have no cash value, and coins earned during the beta reset when MILA launches publicly (see “Coins, Premium, and Billing” in our Terms of Service).

Product analytics

We collect anonymous product-usage events to understand how MILA is used and where people get stuck: when the app is opened, when a sign-up is completed (the sign-in method only — never your email address or name), first votes and first polls, which polls were shown and whether they received a vote or were scrolled past, share and follow taps, counts of comments and story views, and the days on which the app is used on a device. These events are tied to a pseudonymous identifier stored only in our own database. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or the content of your messages or comments for analytics; analytics records do not store IP addresses, and for these product analytics we use no advertising identifiers and no device fingerprinting, and the events are never sent to any third-party analytics or tracking service. Poll results we share or sell are aggregates only, reported for groups large enough that no individual can be identified from them.

This website

This policy also covers the marketing website at mila.milatech.tech itself. The site loads Google AdSense (see ads.txt on this domain), and Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, as described in Google’s own policies; you can control this in your browser settings and Google’s ad settings. Beyond hosting and security logs processed by our hosting provider, and the optional website analytics described below, the website collects nothing about you — the feedback form on this site opens your own email app and stores nothing on our servers.

Website analytics. The marketing website (not the MILA app) uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. If you accept analytics cookies in the site’s cookie notice, Google Analytics collects information such as the pages you visit, how long you spend on the site, your device and browser, your approximate location, and — where Google Signals is available — demographic and interest categories that Google derives from its own data. That information is stored by Google on our behalf and is also exported to our company’s BigQuery data warehouse, where we use it in aggregate to decide what to build and improve. Analytics data is retained for 14 months. If you decline the cookie notice, analytics storage stays off and no analytics data is collected about your visit. Advertising storage is always off. This is separate from the app’s own product analytics described above, which remains first-party only.

Web app analytics. The MILA web app at app.milatech.tech also uses Google Analytics under the same consent rule: nothing is collected unless you accept the cookie notice shown in the web app. If you accept, Google Analytics collects the same kinds of information described above — the pages you visit, how long you spend on them, your device and browser, your approximate location, and, where Google Signals is available, demographic and interest categories Google derives from its own data. That information is stored by Google on our behalf, exported to our company’s BigQuery data warehouse, and retained for 14 months. If you decline the cookie notice, no analytics data is collected about your use of the web app. Advertising storage is always off, and this web analytics never includes the content of your polls, votes, comments, or messages. The native mobile apps do not use Google Analytics; they use only the first-party product analytics described above.

Ads in the web app feed

The feed in the MILA web app (app.milatech.tech) includes cards labeled “Ad” between polls. These cards are served by Google AdSense; when no AdSense ad is available, the same labeled cards promote Mila Tech’s own products instead. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure these ads, as described in Google’s own policies; you can control this in your browser settings and Google’s ad settings.

Direct messages, stories, translation, and media

Direct messages are stored so we can deliver them to the participants and are not shown to other users. Stories and their photos are stored so we can show them to you and the people who follow you; stories expire 24 hours after posting, and deleting a story deletes its photo. Translation covers polls, comments, direct messages, and community posts (not stories). Auto-translate is on by default and can be turned off in Settings; when translation runs, the text is sent to Google Translate. Images you upload are sent to Sightengine so we can moderate them. Video uploads are not enabled in the current beta, so no user video is held by Cloudflare Stream or any other video host.

The images sent to Sightengine are profile avatars, poll cover and option images, and story photos. The check runs once, at upload; under Sightengine’s published terms, images are deleted after processing, and we keep no copy of your images with Sightengine. Translation text is sent to Google Translate only at the moment translation runs.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a device push token and use it to send notifications about activity on your account, such as votes, messages, comments, and community activity. You can turn push notifications off in your device settings, which stops delivery to that device.

A notification about a direct message shows the sender’s name and a preview of the message text (up to 120 characters).

Payments and tips

Purchases are paused during the public beta, and nobody is charged today. When purchases open, iOS payments are processed by Apple, and RevenueCat tells us the resulting entitlement (Premium or a coin pack) without giving us your full card number. Web payments are processed by Stripe, which receives the card details; we receive the payment result, not the full card number. Tips stay paused during the beta. Coins have no cash value.

On the web, Stripe is the processor for Premium subscriptions and coin packs. RevenueCat is used only on iOS and is not part of web checkout. MILA has no Android app today, so Google Play billing is not offered. No processor is connected for tips, and we collect no identity or tax information for them.

3. How We Share Information

  • Other users: your username, polls, aggregated votes, comments, stories (according to the audience you set), and public profile are visible to other users as part of the Service. Every poll also has a share link (app.milatech.tech/p/…): anyone who has a poll’s link can open and view that poll and vote on it as a guest, without an account — treat poll links as public. Direct messages are visible only to participants.
  • Service providers: Supabase (database and hosting), Cloudflare (hosting), Sightengine (image moderation), Google Translate (translation of text you submit), RevenueCat (subscription and purchase status for App Store purchases), Apple (sign-in and App Store payments), Google (sign-in, and Google Analytics with BigQuery export for the website and web-app analytics described in this policy), Stripe (web payments, when purchases open), Google AdMob (ads in the mobile apps, when ads are enabled), and Google AdSense (ads on this website and in the web app feed). Each processes data only to provide its service.
  • Legal: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of users and the Service.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Children

MILA is not directed at children under 13. The minimum age to use MILA is 13. We collect date of birth at sign-up and do not knowingly allow accounts under 13. If we learn a child under 13 has an account, we will delete it. If you are 13 or older but under the age of majority where you live, you may use the Service only with a parent or guardian’s consent. For accounts aged 15 and under, explicit content is hidden and cannot be posted.

We collect date of birth at sign-up to enforce the minimum age. MILA does not collect consent directly from parents or guardians, and sign-up has no parental-consent step.

5. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access / correction: view and update profile information in the app.
  • Deletion: delete your account in the app under Profile → Delete Account, or request deletion of your account and personal data at edward@milatech.tech. Deletion takes effect immediately and includes your profile, the polls and comments you posted, your stories, your votes, the direct messages you sent (also removed from recipients’ inboxes), and your push tokens; your uploaded files are purged from our storage. Direct messages other people sent to you remain in their accounts. Because purchases are paused, there are no payment-processor customer records for your account to delete, and Sightengine received your images only for the one-time moderation check at upload. Copies in automated database backups are overwritten as those backups rotate.
  • Ads: when ads are enabled, free-tier ads use your device advertising identifier; you can limit ad tracking in your device settings. Premium subscribers see no ads.
  • Push notifications: disable anytime in device settings.

6. Data Retention

We keep account and content data while your account is active and as needed to operate the Service, comply with law, and resolve disputes. When you delete your account, the deletion runs immediately as described in Section 5; copies in automated database backups are overwritten as those backups rotate, except where retention is legally required.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted connections, access controls, row-level database security). No system is perfectly secure; you are responsible for keeping your credentials confidential.

8. International Users

The Service is operated in the United States. If you use it from elsewhere, your information is transferred to and processed in the US.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy; material changes will be announced in the app or on our website before they take effect. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.

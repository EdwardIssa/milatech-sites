Referrals and invites

MILA has a referral program. Every account has a referral code, and links you share — including poll links — may carry your code (shown as “?ref=” at the end of the link). After you vote, MILA may offer to let you invite a friend to vote on that poll; sharing is always your choice. When someone opens one of these links and creates an account, we store a referral record — the inviting account, the new account, the code used, and whether the referral has qualified — along with a per-install device identifier used only to prevent abuse of the program, such as one person claiming their own invites. The new account receives 25 coins when it claims the invite, and the inviter receives 50 coins once the new account qualifies by casting 10 votes within 14 days, up to 20 qualified referrals per month. Referral coins are ordinary MILA coins: they have no cash value, and coins earned during the beta reset when MILA launches publicly (see “Coins, Premium, and Billing” in our Terms of Service).

Product analytics

We collect anonymous product-usage events to understand how MILA is used and where people get stuck: when the app is opened, when a sign-up is completed (the sign-in method only — never your email address or name), first votes and first polls, which polls were shown and whether they received a vote or were scrolled past, share and follow taps, counts of comments and story views, and the days on which the app is used on a device. These events are tied to a pseudonymous identifier stored only in our own database. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or the content of your messages or comments for analytics; analytics records do not store IP addresses, and for these product analytics we use no advertising identifiers and no device fingerprinting, and the events are never sent to any third-party analytics or tracking service. Poll results we share or sell are aggregates only, reported for groups large enough that no individual can be identified from them.

This website

This policy also covers the marketing website at mila.milatech.tech itself. The site loads Google AdSense (see ads.txt on this domain), and Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, as described in Google’s own policies; you can control this in your browser settings and Google’s ad settings. Beyond hosting and security logs processed by our hosting provider, and the optional website analytics described below, the website collects nothing about you — the feedback form on this site opens your own email app and stores nothing on our servers.

Website analytics. The marketing website (not the MILA app) uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. If you accept analytics cookies in the site’s cookie notice, Google Analytics collects information such as the pages you visit, how long you spend on the site, your device and browser, your approximate location, and — where Google Signals is available — demographic and interest categories that Google derives from its own data. That information is stored by Google on our behalf and is also exported to our company’s BigQuery data warehouse, where we use it in aggregate to decide what to build and improve. Analytics data is retained for 14 months. If you decline the cookie notice, analytics storage stays off and no analytics data is collected about your visit. Advertising storage is always off. This is separate from the app’s own product analytics described above, which remains first-party only.

Web app analytics. The MILA web app at app.milatech.tech also uses Google Analytics under the same consent rule: nothing is collected unless you accept the cookie notice shown in the web app. If you accept, Google Analytics collects the same kinds of information described above — the pages you visit, how long you spend on them, your device and browser, your approximate location, and, where Google Signals is available, demographic and interest categories Google derives from its own data. That information is stored by Google on our behalf, exported to our company’s BigQuery data warehouse, and retained for 14 months. If you decline the cookie notice, no analytics data is collected about your use of the web app. Advertising storage is always off, and this web analytics never includes the content of your polls, votes, comments, or messages. The native mobile apps do not use Google Analytics; they use only the first-party product analytics described above.

Ads in the web app feed

The feed in the MILA web app (app.milatech.tech) includes cards labeled “Ad” between polls. These cards are served by Google AdSense; when no AdSense ad is available, the same labeled cards promote Mila Tech’s own products instead. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure these ads, as described in Google’s own policies; you can control this in your browser settings and Google’s ad settings.

Direct messages, stories, translation, and media

Direct messages are stored so we can deliver them to the participants and are not shown to other users. Stories and their photos are stored so we can show them to the audience selected in the app; deleting a story deletes its photo. If you use translation, the text being translated is sent to Google Translate. Images you upload are sent to Sightengine so we can moderate them. Video, when the product offers it, is hosted by Cloudflare Stream. During the current beta, photos are the supported poll cover and video in polls is limited.

Payments, tips, and payouts

Purchases are paused during the public beta, and nobody is charged today. When purchases open, iOS payments are processed by Apple, and RevenueCat tells us the resulting entitlement (Premium or a coin pack) without giving us your full card number. Web payments are processed by Stripe, which receives the card details; we receive the payment result, not the full card number. Tips and prize payouts stay paused during the beta. Coins have no cash value and are not a payout.

For accounts aged 15 and under, explicit content is hidden and cannot be posted.

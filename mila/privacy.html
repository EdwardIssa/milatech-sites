3. How We Share Information

  • Other users: your username, polls, aggregated votes, comments, and public profile are visible to other users as part of the Service. Direct messages are visible only to participants.
  • Service providers: Supabase (database & hosting), RevenueCat (subscription/purchase status), Google AdMob (ads on the free tier), Apple/Google (sign-in and payments). Each processes data only to provide its service.
  • Legal: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of users and the Service.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Children

MILA is not directed at children under 13. We collect date of birth at sign-up and do not knowingly allow accounts under 13. If we learn a child under 13 has an account, we will delete it.

5. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access / correction: view and update profile information in the app.
  • Deletion: request deletion of your account and personal data at milatechnogies@gmail.com. Publicly posted content (polls, comments) may remain in aggregated or anonymized form.
  • Ads: free-tier ads use your device advertising identifier; you can limit ad tracking in your device settings. Premium subscribers see no ads.
  • Push notifications: disable anytime in device settings.

6. Data Retention

We keep account and content data while your account is active and as needed to operate the Service, comply with law, and resolve disputes. Deleted accounts are removed or anonymized within a reasonable period, except where retention is legally required.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted connections, access controls, row-level database security). No system is perfectly secure; you are responsible for keeping your credentials confidential.

8. International Users

The Service is operated in the United States. If you use it from elsewhere, your information is transferred to and processed in the US.

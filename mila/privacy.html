Product analytics

We collect anonymous product-usage events to understand how MILA is used and where people get stuck: when the app is opened, when a sign-up is completed (the sign-in method only — never your email address or name), first votes and first polls, which polls were shown and whether they received a vote or were scrolled past, share and follow taps, counts of comments and story views, and the days on which the app is used on a device. These events are tied to a pseudonymous identifier stored only in our own database. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or the content of your messages or comments for analytics; analytics records do not store IP addresses, and we use no advertising identifiers for analytics, no device fingerprinting, and no third-party analytics or tracking services. Poll results we share or sell are aggregates only, reported for groups large enough that no individual can be identified from them.

This website

This policy also covers the marketing website at mila.milatech.tech itself. The site loads Google AdSense (see ads.txt on this domain), and Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, as described in Google’s own policies; you can control this in your browser settings and Google’s ad settings. This website does not run a visit counter. Beyond hosting and security logs processed by our hosting provider, the website collects nothing about you — the feedback form on this site opens your own email app and stores nothing on our servers.

3. How We Share Information

  • Other users: your username, polls, aggregated votes, comments, and public profile are visible to other users as part of the Service. Direct messages are visible only to participants.
  • Service providers: Supabase (database & hosting), RevenueCat (subscription/purchase status), Google AdMob (ads on the free tier, when ads are enabled), Apple/Google (sign-in and payments). Each processes data only to provide its service.
  • Legal: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of users and the Service.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Children

MILA is not directed at children under 13. We collect date of birth at sign-up and do not knowingly allow accounts under 13. If we learn a child under 13 has an account, we will delete it. For accounts aged 15 and under, explicit content is hidden and cannot be posted.

5. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access / correction: view and update profile information in the app.
  • Deletion: delete your account in the app under Profile → Delete Account, or request deletion of your account and personal data at edward@milatech.tech. Publicly posted content (polls, comments) may remain in aggregated or anonymized form.
  • Ads: when ads are enabled, free-tier ads use your device advertising identifier; you can limit ad tracking in your device settings. Premium subscribers see no ads.
  • Push notifications: disable anytime in device settings.

6. Data Retention

We keep account and content data while your account is active and as needed to operate the Service, comply with law, and resolve disputes. Deleted accounts are removed or anonymized within a reasonable period, except where retention is legally required.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted connections, access controls, row-level database security). No system is perfectly secure; you are responsible for keeping your credentials confidential.

8. International Users

The Service is operated in the United States. If you use it from elsewhere, your information is transferred to and processed in the US.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy; material changes will be announced in the app or on our website before they take effect. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.

4. The Service

MILA lets you create and vote in polls in seven formats — Quick (Yes / Neutral / No), Custom, Quiz, Multi-select, Ranked, Slider, and Q&A — comment, send direct messages, post stories, join communities, earn leaderboard placement, and earn or purchase coins. Direct messages. Signed-in members can send direct messages to other members. Guest voting does not include messaging. A direct message is visible only to the people in that conversation. The community rules in Section 8 apply to messages the same way they apply to public posts.

Stories. Signed-in members can post stories, including photos. A story is visible to you and to people who follow you, and every story expires 24 hours after it is posted. You can delete a story you posted, and deleting it also deletes its photo. Blocks, mutes, and bans apply to stories.

Translation. MILA can translate polls, comments, direct messages, and community posts using Google Translate. Auto-translate is on by default and can be turned off in Settings; on some screens you tap a translate option instead. When text is translated, it is sent to Google Translate, which returns the translation. Stories are not translated.

Photos and image moderation. You can attach images, including poll covers, poll option images, story photos, and a profile avatar. Those images are sent to Sightengine, an image-moderation service, for a one-time check at upload, so we can detect and block content that violates these Terms. Video uploads are not enabled in the current beta.

Push notifications. If you allow notifications, we may send push notifications about activity on your account, such as votes, messages, comments, and community activity. We use a device push token to deliver them. You can turn notifications off in your device settings at any time.

For a direct message, the notification shows the sender’s name and a preview of the message text.

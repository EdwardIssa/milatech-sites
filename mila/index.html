Questions, answered

The short version.

Is the beta really live?

Yes. The web beta is live right now at app.milatech.tech — start voting, no invite needed. We're also rolling out city by city, Detroit first, through an invite-only waitlist: join here and we'll open the doors for you as soon as your wave starts. The native iOS and Android apps are on the way.

What makes MILA different?

MILA is opinion-first social. Every poll is live, every vote feels electric, and everything — DMs, hashtags, streaks, leaderboards — is built to keep the conversation moving. It's not a feed you scroll past; it's a feed you argue with.

Is MILA really free?

Yes. The free tier includes unlimited 3-hour and 24-hour polls, monthly flash polls, voting on everything, DMs, hashtags, communities, and coin rewards. Premium ($2.99/mo or $19.99/yr) unlocks all durations and formats, more flash polls, and removes ads.

What are coins?

Coins are MILA's in-app currency. You earn them by voting and creating polls, or buy coin packs. Spend them on extra flash polls, visibility boosts, and cover images. Coins have no cash value and can't be transferred.

What is a flash poll?

A poll that runs for just 1 or 5 minutes — built for instant, real-time reactions. Everyone gets monthly flash polls included; after that, spend coins (or watch a rewarded ad on the free tier).

Who can use MILA?

MILA is for ages 13 and up. We collect your date of birth at sign-up, filter content by age, and block explicit content entirely for users 15 and under. Block, mute, and report tools are one tap away.

How is my data handled?

See our Privacy Policy. We don't sell your personal information, and you can request deletion of your account and data anytime.

New in the makeover

A new look — deep ink, brand gradient — and the signup wall is gone. Four things changed how MILA works:

Guest voting — no signup

Someone sends you a poll link, you tap your side, you’re done. No account, no password, no wall. Voting from a link just works — try it on any live poll.

The Daily One

One big question, every day, that everybody votes on. Standings stay masked while it runs — the reveal lands at 9 PM Detroit time.

Group-link polls

Any poll can be a group link: send it to your people, they vote as guests, and the group settles it. Built for the debates your group chat never finishes.

Result cards

Every result becomes a shareable card — a clean image of the final split, made to drop straight into the group chat or your story.

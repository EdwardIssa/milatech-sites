03 / Honest limits

What DRAE does — and does not — claim

  • Proven: when one expert progressively corrupts, DRAE cuts prediction error by 85.6% vs a static equal-weight ensemble in its reproducible benchmark (91.9% in the second half).
  • Documented limit: when every expert fails identically at once, disagreement carries no signal and DRAE shows no gain in that scenario. That limit is printed in the shipped results file, next to the win.
  • Pre-revenue asset: no customers, ARR or production deployments are claimed. DRAE is working, tested software offered as code + IP — priced and sold as exactly that.
  • Runs locally: a Python library (NumPy / SciPy / scikit-learn, optional Redis telemetry). Your models and data stay in your environment.

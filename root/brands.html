01 / What a sponsored poll is

Your question, MILA’s audience, real answers

MILA is a real-time polling app: people vote in one tap, standings move live, and results are public by design. A sponsored poll is your question run through that same machinery — clearly labeled Sponsored in the feed, open to MILA members and to guests, and counted the same way as every other poll on the platform.

One question, asked well

You bring the question — a product call, a policy stance, a ticker, a take — or we draft it with you. Wording stays neutral: MILA doesn’t pick a side, the audience does. Formats range from a one-tap quick poll to sliders, rankings, multi-select, and quizzes.

Real votes, labeled as yours

Every sponsored poll carries a Sponsored label in the app, and votes are cast by real people — signed-in members and guests voting through embeds — one person, one vote. MILA never seeds, buys, or models engagement, so the number in your report is the number that actually happened.

Live results you can publish

Standings update in real time on a public results page, and the poll itself can be embedded — live and guest-votable — on your own site or article, the same way the poll in our guest-voting Insights piece runs inside this site. When the run closes, you get the sentiment report: full option breakdown, member/guest split, and the takeaway.

02 / How it works

Four steps from question to report

01Bring the question

Tell us what you need to know and who it’s for. We shape it into one neutral, answerable poll question and pick the format that fits — quick vote, slider, ranked, or multi-select.

02We launch it, labeled

Your poll goes live in the MILA feed with a Sponsored label, on your schedule — a flash run or a multi-day window — and, if you want it, embedded live on your own pages.

03Answers stream in live

Members and guests vote in one tap. You watch the standings move in real time on the public results page while the poll runs — no waiting for a fielding window to close.

04You get the sentiment report

At close: the full breakdown by option with counts and percentages, the member/guest split, the response window, and a plain-language takeaway — formatted to quote, publish, or drop into a deck, like the sample below.

03 / Sample sentiment report

A real poll, reported the way yours will be

This is an actual MILA poll with its actual results — the same poll that runs, embedded and guest-votable, in our Insights article on guest voting. It is shown to demonstrate the report format with real numbers, not to claim scale.

Sample report · Politics

“Should Congress be banned from trading stocks?”

2 responses · 1 MILA member + 1 guest (via web embed) · responses recorded Oct 23 and Oct 26, 2026 · poll live Oct 23 – Oct 30, 2026 · see the live poll

Yes, ban it outright · 1 vote · 50%

Allow it, but disclose every trade in real time · 1 vote · 50%

No, it’s their money too · 0 votes · 0%

The takeaway: both responses favored new restrictions on congressional trading — one an outright ban, one real-time disclosure — and the permissive option drew no support at all. At n = 2 that is a format sample, not a finding; a sponsored run reports the same structure at whatever size your audience delivers.

Read this honestly: MILA is in open beta — 11 members today — so live counts are small, and this sample shows them exactly as they are. We would rather show you a real 2 than an invented 20,000. Sponsored reports never pad, seed, or simulate responses.

Legal / Privacy

Privacy Policy — milatech.tech

Effective date: November 8, 2026 · Mila Tech LLC, a Delaware limited liability company operated from Troy, Michigan, United States · Contact: edward@milatech.tech

This policy covers the company website milatech.tech only. MILA, Owed, DRAE, Loyal, and LeadMiner X each have their own privacy policies, which govern those products.

This policy describes what information this Site collects, why, and what we never do with it. It is written to match how the Site actually works — including our standing public commitment: this business is funded by its products, plus clearly-labeled ads on its free sites — never by selling user data. We do not sell personal information. We never have, and this policy does not create an exception.

1. Information you give us

Contact / lead form. The Site’s contact form (on the Contact and Services pages) asks for your name, work email, company or organization, the topic of your inquiry (a category you select), optionally the size of your team or audience, and your message. When you submit it, your submission is transmitted to and stored in our lead database (operated by us on Cloudflare infrastructure, through the same lead service our Owed product uses), together with a timestamp and an audit record of its receipt; or, if that service is unreachable, the form instead offers to open your own email application with the same details prefilled, so you can send them to us by email — in that case nothing is transmitted to us until you send the email yourself.

Email. If you email us directly, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

What we use it for: to read and respond to your inquiry (our stated target is a response within two business days), to discuss the work or partnership you asked about, and to keep a record of the correspondence. We do not add form submitters to marketing lists, and we do not send bulk email to people who contacted us.

Please do not submit Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, passwords, or health information through the form or by email — nothing on this Site needs them.

2. Information collected automatically

No visit counter. This Site does not run a visit counter and does not count or profile individual visitors. We do not set our own tracking or analytics cookies.

Hosting and security logs. The Site is hosted on Cloudflare. As with essentially every website, the hosting and content-delivery infrastructure processes technical information necessary to deliver pages and protect the service — such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps — under the provider’s own policies. We receive aggregate, non-identifying statistics (such as total visits by page and country) from the hosting platform.

Advertising technology. This Site loads Google AdSense. When ads are active on the Site, Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted, as described in Google’s own policies. You can control this through your browser’s cookie settings and Google’s ad settings (adssettings.google.com). Advertising does not change what we do with information you give us: we do not provide your form submissions or emails to advertisers, and we do not sell personal information to anyone.

Embedded product content. One or more articles on the Site embed a live MILA poll served from app.milatech.tech. Interacting with embedded content is governed by MILA’s Privacy Policy, just as if you visited that product directly.

3. How we use information — the complete list

  • Responding to inquiries and conducting the business those inquiries start.
  • Operating, securing, and improving the Site.
  • Serving clearly-labeled advertising on the free Site.
  • Complying with legal obligations and enforcing our Terms of Use.

We do not use information collected by this Site to build advertising profiles, and we do not combine it with data from our products (each product handles its own data under its own policy).

4. Sharing

We share personal information only: with the service providers that make the Site work (currently Cloudflare for hosting and lead storage, and Google for advertising), each acting under its own terms and policies and only as needed to provide its service; if required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Mila Tech, our users, or others; or in connection with a sale or reorganization of the business, in which case this policy or a successor policy at least as protective will continue to apply to information collected by the Site. That is the entire list. There is no “partners” category, and there is no sale of personal information — to data brokers or anyone else.

5. Retention

Lead-form submissions and emails: retained while your inquiry or any resulting business relationship is active, and for a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping (generally no longer than three years after the last contact), then deleted. You may ask us to delete yours sooner at any time (see §7). Hosting logs: retained by the provider under its own schedule.

6. Children

The Site is a business website aimed at adults and organizations. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 through it. If you believe a child has submitted information through the Site, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices and rights

You may, at any time, by emailing edward@milatech.tech: ask what personal information we hold that you submitted through this Site; ask us to correct it or delete it (we will confirm deletion once done); or withdraw consent to be contacted about an inquiry. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy law (such as rights to access, deletion, and to opt out of “sale” — noting that we do not sell personal information at all). We honor deletion and access requests from any visitor, wherever they live. You can also control cookies and advertising personalization in your browser and in Google’s ad settings at any time; the Site’s content remains available if you block them.

8. Security

Lead submissions are transmitted over HTTPS and stored with access limited to Mila Tech. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security — but we collect very little through this Site, by design.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change what the Site collects or how it’s used, we will update this policy, revise its effective date, and — for material changes — note the change on the Site. The “never sell user data” commitment in the introduction is a standing company commitment: if it ever changed, this policy would say so in plain language at the top, before any change took effect.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: edward@milatech.tech

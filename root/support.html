01 / Get help

Get help by product

The fastest route to an answer is the product’s own home. Pick where your question lives:

Live beta

MILA — polling beta

For beta questions, start at mila.milatech.tech. Known issues and fixes are published on MILA’s public Fix Tracker there, so check whether your issue is already logged — and fixed — before writing in. To report a bug or request a feature, use the MILA feedback page; feedback is triaged into the public Fix Tracker, where you can watch it move.

Company

Company & general

For partnerships, services engagements, press, or anything that does not belong to one product, write to edward@milatech.tech or use the contact form, which routes your message by topic — services, MILA for Brands, Owed Enterprise, DRAE pilots, or general — so it lands in the right queue the first time.

02 / Response expectations

What happens after you write

Business inquiries — services, enterprise, pilots, partnerships, and press — are answered within 2 business days. That is a promise sized to a one-person operation that intends to keep it; if a reply needs work on our side first, you will get an acknowledgment and a real timeline instead of silence. MILA beta feedback follows a different, more public path: reports submitted through the feedback page are triaged into the public Fix Tracker on mila.milatech.tech, where fixes are logged round by round — 447+ issues found and fixed so far — so the status of your report is something you can check, not something you have to ask about. For Owed, claim and deadline questions are answered in the context of your own hunt and tracker at owed.milatech.tech; we will never ask you to send a Social Security number or a password by email, and you should never send either.

03 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is MILA free?

Yes. Voting in the MILA beta at app.milatech.tech is free — guests can vote with no signup at all. MILA Premium, at $2.99 per month or $19.99 per year, removes limits and ads and unlocks every poll duration, and optional coin packs (50 for $0.99 up to 1,200 for $7.99) power 1-minute and 5-minute flash polls.

How does Owed make money — and can I file on my own for free?

Yes, you always can: legitimate claim filing is free, every Owed find shows its official source and a free do-it-yourself path, and no claim is ever filed without your per-claim approval. Owed charges a $6.99-per-month subscription for the work around filing — continuous hunting across settlements and unclaimed property, intake-first claim preparation, deadline tracking, and alerts. Owed never collects your Social Security number.

Is DRAE for sale? Can we run a pilot instead?

DRAE is offered for acquisition at $2,500 with full IP assignment — a complete Python codebase with 19 passing tests and a reproducible benchmark (85.6% lower error than a static ensemble when one model corrupts). If a pilot or live demo is the right first step for your team, book one through drae.milatech.tech and evaluate the working system before deciding.

How does a services engagement start?

With the contact form or an email to edward@milatech.tech describing the workflow, diagnostic, or product problem. That leads to a working call, then a scoped quote — deliverables, timeline, and price fixed before work begins. Mila Tech does not publish an hourly rate card; automation, ML and predictive diagnostics, and product engineering engagements are each scoped on what they have to deliver.

Where is Mila Tech located and registered?

Mila Tech LLC is a Delaware limited liability company (Delaware file number 10721454, formed August 3, 2026), operated from Troy, Michigan, in Metro Detroit. Company email at edward@milatech.tech has been live on Google Workspace since October 4, 2026.

How are community donations used?

Donations go straight into MILA’s development: servers, store launches, and the features the community asks for. The counter on this page is honest — it reads $0 raised so far against a $1,000 founding goal — and it only moves when a real donation lands. Supporting the products directly, by subscribing or joining an enterprise pilot, helps the same work.

Support

02 / Response expectations

What happens after you write

Status & known issues: MILA’s known issues, fixes, and current beta status are public on the MILA Fix Tracker — check there first; if your issue is listed, its fix status is too. For this company site itself, report a problem by email and we will confirm receipt. Business inquiries — services, enterprise, pilots, partnerships, and press — are answered within 2 business days. Product enquiries are also answered by email — write to the product route above or to edward@milatech.tech; a one-person operation answers them in the same queue, and MILA beta bug reports are answered publicly in the Fix Tracker rather than by private reply. That is a promise sized to a one-person operation that intends to keep it; if a reply needs work on our side first, you will get an acknowledgment and a real timeline instead of silence. MILA beta feedback follows a different, more public path: reports submitted through the feedback page are triaged into the public Fix Tracker on mila.milatech.tech, where fixes are logged round by round — 647 issues found and fixed so far — so the status of your report is something you can check, not something you have to ask about. For Owed, claim and deadline questions are answered in the context of your own hunt and tracker at owed.milatech.tech; we will never ask you to send a Social Security number or a password by email, and you should never send either.

03 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is MILA free?

Yes. Voting in the MILA beta at app.milatech.tech is free — guests can vote with no signup at all. MILA Premium, at $2.99 per month or $19.99 per year, removes limits and ads and unlocks every poll duration, and optional coin packs (50 for $0.99 up to 1,200 for $7.99) power 1-minute and 5-minute flash polls. Beta caveat: while MILA is in beta, purchases are paused and everyone gets the full experience free while we test — nobody is charged today.

How does Owed make money — and can I file on my own for free?

Yes, you always can: legitimate claim filing is free, every Owed find shows its official source and a free do-it-yourself path, and no claim is ever filed without your per-claim approval. Owed Premium is a $6.99-per-month subscription for the work around filing — it launches soon, so billing is off and nobody is charged for it today — continuous hunting across settlements and unclaimed property, intake-first claim preparation, deadline tracking, and alerts. Owed never collects your Social Security number.

Is DRAE for sale? Can we run a pilot instead?

DRAE is offered for acquisition at $2,500 with full IP assignment — a complete Python codebase with 19 passing tests and a reproducible benchmark (85.6% lower error than a static ensemble when one model corrupts). If a pilot or live demo is the right first step for your team, book one through drae.milatech.tech and evaluate the working system before deciding.

How does a services engagement start?

With the contact form or an email to edward@milatech.tech describing the workflow, diagnostic, or product problem. That leads to a working call, then a scoped quote — deliverables, timeline, and price fixed before work begins. Mila Tech does not publish an hourly rate card; automation, ML and predictive diagnostics, and product engineering engagements are each scoped on what they have to deliver.

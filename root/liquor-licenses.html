The licenses

Know which one you actually need

SDD

Spirits to take home in sealed bottles. This is the liquor store license, and the one most buyers are really after.

SDM

Beer and wine to take home. Often paired with an SDD, and the license most party stores and markets already hold.

Class C

Drinks by the glass, served and consumed on site. Bars, restaurants, and banquet halls. A different license, a different market.

Escrow

A license parked at the state, inactive. Escrowed licenses rarely get advertised, and they are often where the real deals are.

Prices swing hard by county because the state caps how many licenses each area gets. A license in a capped-out metro county and the same license in a rural county are different worlds, and offering one county's number in another is how people overpay or get ignored.

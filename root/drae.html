Summary

DRAE, in one paragraph

DRAE is a working Python library that governs ML ensembles and AI agents by expert disagreement: it measures how much your models disagree on each live decision, re-weights failing models, and halts execution fail-closed when the ensemble stops agreeing with itself — with an audit trail your compliance team can read. It ships with tests and a reproducible benchmark, and it is offered for acquisition at $2,500 with full IP assignment (subscription plans for teams that license it instead are published on its own site). This page is a summary; the full offer — demos, benchmark, plans, and the sale details — lives on DRAE’s own site. Status as of Nov 2026.

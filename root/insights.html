Insights

What we learn by building.

Insights is where Mila Tech thinks in public: product thinking, build-in-public engineering notes, and category pieces on the problems our products exist to solve. Everything here is grounded in work we actually ship — a live polling app, a money-recovery service, and a drift-governance library — and every claim is one we can point to in a product, a process, or a public record.

01 / Why we publish

Notes from a working shop, not a content farm

We publish on a simple rule: if we have not built it, run it, filed it, or measured it, we do not write about it as if we have. That keeps this section small, honest, and useful. New pieces arrive as the work produces them — when a product decision teaches us something, when a filing process reveals a pattern worth sharing, or when an engineering trade-off turns out to matter beyond our own codebase. If you are facing the same problem, Edward’s hope — he built these products and wrote these pieces — is that you leave with a method you can use, not just an opinion you can forget.

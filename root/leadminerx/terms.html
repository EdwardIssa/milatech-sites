What LeadMiner X is. LeadMiner X is a desktop application that queries Google’s official Places API using your own Google API key and exports business listings to CSV files on your own computer — and, for businesses with a website, the email address the business itself publishes on that site. It includes a free trial mode (25 leads) and a paid license that unlocks mining with no LeadMiner X cap. Google’s per-request rate still applies.

Your license. A purchase grants you a license to install and use LeadMiner X on your own machines for your own business use. The license includes all updates within the same major version line as your purchase (for example, all v4.x updates for a v4 purchase) at no additional fee within that line. The license is not for resale, sharing, sublicensing, or running a list-selling service for third parties.

Price and payment. LeadMiner X is sold for a one-time payment through Gumroad at the price shown at checkout (the launch price published on the product page is $149). There is no subscription and no per-result fee charged by us. Trial downloads are provided free through a separate Gumroad listing.

Refunds. If LeadMiner X doesn’t work on your machine or isn’t what you expected, email us within 14 days of purchase for a full refund. Refunded or charged-back license keys stop working.

Your Google account and API costs. LeadMiner X is a bring-your-own-key tool. You supply your own Google Cloud account and API key.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026. This policy covers LeadMiner X, a desktop application by Mila Tech LLC, and the LeadMiner X website.

The short version. LeadMiner X runs on your computer. There is no LeadMiner X server collecting your searches, your API key, or your leads. We never see them.

What stays on your machine. Your Google API key is stored locally on your computer and used only to call Google from your computer. Your searches go straight from your computer to Google. Your CSV exports and your lead-history (dedup) database are written to your disk. None of this passes through us, and we cannot access it.

What Google processes. Your searches are processed by Google under your own Google Cloud account, under Google’s terms and privacy policy. For leads with a website, the app also reads the business’s own public website to find the email address the business itself publishes.

License checks. To activate a license and confirm it remains valid, the app checks your license key with Gumroad, our payment and licensing provider. That check sends the license key to Gumroad. It does not send your searches, leads, or API key anywhere.

What we receive. When you buy or download LeadMiner X, Gumroad processes the transaction and shares order details with us (such as your email address and license key) so we can deliver the product and provide support. If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use this only to deliver the product, provide support, and meet legal and accounting obligations. We do not sell personal information.

This website. The marketing page is static. It sets no cookies of its own. If a cookie notice is shown and you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics collects information such as the pages you visit, how long you spend on the site, your device and browser, your approximate location, and — where Google Signals is available — demographic and interest categories that Google derives from its own data. That information is stored by Google on our behalf and is also exported to our company’s BigQuery data warehouse, where we use it in aggregate to decide what to build and improve. Analytics data is retained for 14 months. If you decline the cookie notice, or if the page you are on does not ask, analytics storage stays off. Advertising storage is always off. None of this changes the app: LeadMiner X still runs on your computer, as described above.

Anonymous usage statistics (opt-in). Beginning with version 4.1, LeadMiner X asks — once, the first time that version runs — whether you are willing to share anonymous usage statistics that help us decide what to build next. Sharing is off unless you choose “Share anonymous stats” in that dialog. If you decline, or never answer, nothing is sent, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Functions → “Usage statistics.” If you opt in, the app periodically sends small reports containing only: the app version and your operating system; when a search runs, whether it was a single, bulk, or deep-sweep search, how many leads it found, and a rough duration range; which of the nine lead fields you have switched on; exports (the format and how many leads); whether the free-trial limit was reached; whether a license was activated; and the names of settings you change (never their values). Reports are tied to a random identifier generated on your computer when you first opt in. It is not derived from your name, your account, your machine, or anything else about you, and it is the only identifier we receive. We never receive — and the reports are technically incapable of containing — your leads or any data about the businesses you find (names, phone numbers, email addresses, websites, or street addresses), your search keywords or locations, your Google Places API key, your license key, your Gumroad account or email address, your file names or paths, or your IP address (our collection server stores none). On our side the reports are immediately folded into per-install counters; no raw report log is kept. We use the counters only in aggregate — how many searches ran, how many leads were mined in total, which fields people leave on — to guide development and to describe the product honestly. Turning statistics off in Settings stops sending immediately and deletes any reports still waiting unsent on your computer. If you would also like the counters already recorded under your install’s random identifier deleted, email edward@milatech.tech and we will remove them.

Your choices. You can delete the app and its local data (your API key, lead history, and CSVs) from your computer at any time. To ask what order information we hold about you, or to have it deleted where the law allows, email us.

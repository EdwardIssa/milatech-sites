Owed — Money-recovery, automated

Status as of Nov 2026

Owed finds money people are already owed — class-action settlements and state unclaimed property — matches it honestly by tier (Verified / Likely / Possible, never blended), and prepares filings intake-first with per-claim approval and a free DIY path on every find. Consumer subscription is $6.99/mo at launch — Owed Premium launches soon, billing is off today, and nobody is charged for it yet; the first hunt is free and property search is always free.

For business: Owed Enterprise brings the hunt to employers as a benefit, to credit unions and fintechs as member engagement (white-label/API), and to tax pros each filing season.

LOYAL — Know who’s really out there

Status as of Nov 2026

The category’s better version: username, email, and name searches across public profiles on the social web, with phone lookups handled through guided public links rather than a direct phone search — run on public data only, reported in honest tiers (Verified / Likely / Possible, sources shown), with no ad trackers and no data selling. Anonymous searching is stateless; signed-in users’ search history is retained for them, as Loyal’s own policy discloses. Every search is free; the first one to run is yourself.

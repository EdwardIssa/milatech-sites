Summary

LOYAL, in one paragraph

LOYAL is a public profile search: give it a username, an email, or a name and it checks public profiles live across 15 platforms, reporting matches in honest tiers (Verified / Likely / Possible) with sources shown — public data only, no ad trackers, no data selling. Email search checks the public profile tied to that email (Gravatar) — it does not search breached databases. Anonymous searching is stateless; signed-in users’ search history is retained for them, as Loyal’s own privacy policy discloses. Search and the verdict summary are always free; full report details and monitoring are paid. Dating coverage is stated plainly on the product site: Tinder is the only dating platform with publicly verifiable profiles; the walled apps are named with the reason rather than given an invented verdict. Phone numbers are not searched directly; phone lookups run through guided public links from the product site. Photo Match is in development and is not listed as a search until it ships. This page is a summary; the live checker and full details live on Loyal’s own site. Status as of Nov 2026.

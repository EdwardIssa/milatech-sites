02 / How it works

Three steps, no mystery

  • 1 · Tell us what you knowUsername, email, name, or phone

    One starting point is enough. You’re never asked for the other person’s password, account access, or anything private — because LOYAL never touches anything private.

  • 2 · LOYAL searches public sources onlyPublic data only

    Public profiles across the social web, checked live. Nothing hacked, nothing leaked, nothing bought from data brokers — if it isn’t public, it isn’t in the report.

  • 3 · A report with honest tiersVerified / Likely / Possible

    Every match is tiered — Verified, Likely, or Possible, never blended — with the source shown for each one, so you can judge the evidence yourself. And “we found nothing” is a complete answer, delivered as one.

03 / Built different

The better version, on purpose

We studied the category before building LOYAL. Here is what the incumbents do — and what we do instead.

01Public data only

No breached databases, no stalkerware, no live location. If a source isn’t public, LOYAL doesn’t use it — full stop.

02Honest answers

Match tiers with sources attached. A “possible” never dresses up as a “verified,” and a no-match is reported as a no-match.

03No tracker soup

The best-known site in this category loads its pages wrapped in ad trackers and click IDs. LOYAL runs clean — no ad trackers, and your searches are never sold. Ever.

04Flat, published pricing

One flat price per report, published before launch. No subscription traps, no credit-pack maze, no “results unlocked” upsell ladder.

05Private by design

Searches don’t notify the person searched, and search history isn’t a product we sell to anyone else.

06A real opt-out

Listed somewhere you shouldn’t be? Anyone can request removal of their information and it will be honored — the same standard we hold our other products to.

04 / Straight answers

Asked, answered

Is this legal?

LOYAL only searches information that is already public — public profiles and public records. It does not access private accounts, intercept messages, or track anyone’s location.

Will the person know I searched them?

No. LOYAL searches don’t send notifications or leave a “viewed your profile” trace from you.

What if LOYAL finds nothing?

Then the report says exactly that — and a clean no-match is real information, not a failed search dressed up as one.

Can I remove myself?

Yes. Anyone can request removal of their information from LOYAL results, and removals are honored. Contact us and it gets done.

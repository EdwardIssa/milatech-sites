02 / Service line one

Mortgage workflow automation

High-volume wholesale mortgage front-end operations run on repetition: the same lookups, the same status checks, the same data moved between the same screens, hundreds of times a day, in a 9-to-6 calling environment where every manual minute is a minute not spent with a broker or a borrower. Mila Tech builds production browser automation for exactly that floor — custom userscript and Tampermonkey-style tooling in the Vicidial tradition that sits inside the tools your team already uses and removes the repetitive work from their day.

This is automation built by someone who has done the manual job. That matters, because the difference between a script that demos well and a tool that survives a production queue is knowing which steps are truly mechanical, which ones carry judgment, and which ones must never be automated at all. We map the workflow first, automate the mechanical layer, and leave clear human checkpoints where compliance, licensing, or judgment requires a person.

Deliverables typically include a workflow and friction map of the target process; production userscripts or browser tooling installed and tested in your environment; guardrails and human-checkpoint design for regulated steps; an hours-saved measurement comparing before and after on your own volumes; and operator documentation plus training notes so the tool keeps working when we are not in the room. Engagements are compliance-aware by design: we work only with material you are authorized to share, we never require or reuse confidential employer material from any other operation, and borrower-data handling follows your own policies and systems of record.

Who it is for: wholesale and retail mortgage operations teams, loan officer assistants and their managers, and any high-throughput service operation whose people are spending skilled hours on unskilled repetition. How it runs: we start by shadowing the real workflow on a working call, identify the highest-volume friction, and pilot the automation on one process before anything scales.

03 / Service line two

Machine learning & predictive diagnostics

Models in production do not fail all at once — they drift, disagree, and quietly get worse while dashboards still look green. Mila Tech’s second service line applies machine learning where it earns its keep: predictive diagnostics over real telemetry, and governance for the ensembles and agents making decisions in production.

Our proof is a product we built and offer today. DRAE is a working Python library that governs ML ensembles and AI agents by expert disagreement — re-weighting failing models and halting execution fail-closed when the ensemble no longer agrees with itself. It ships with 19 passing tests and a reproducible benchmark showing 85.6% lower error than a static ensemble when one model corrupts. DRAE is pre-revenue and labeled as such everywhere we describe it; the benchmark, not a sales claim, is the evidence.

The same thinking drives our automotive work: machine learning over CAN-bus telemetry to predict component failures before they occur — the car tells you what breaks next, before it breaks. Deliverables in this line include drift and disagreement monitoring design for models you already run; ensemble governance pilots using the DRAE approach on your own evaluation data; predictive-diagnostics prototypes that connect telemetry to a measurable failure prediction; model-monitoring dashboards and alert rules tied to pre-registered decision thresholds; and an audit trail your risk, compliance, or leadership team can actually read — who decided a model was still fit to run, on what evidence, and when.

Who it is for: teams running ML in production without a named owner for drift, operations with rich sensor or telemetry data and no prediction layer on top of it, and builders of AI-agent systems who need a fail-closed halt before an agent acts on a bad consensus. How it runs: we begin with your data and your current monitoring, agree the signals and thresholds in writing, and prove the approach on a fixed-scope pilot before any wider rollout.

Ensemble drift governance CAN-bus predictive diagnostics Fail-closed agent halts Auditable model decisions

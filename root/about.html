About

A builder-led technology company.

Mila Tech LLC is a Delaware limited liability company based in Metro Detroit, Michigan. We build consumer software, workflow automation, and intelligent systems — end to end, from cloud backend to physical hardware.

01 / The company

Find friction. Engineer it out.

Mila Tech’s loop is simple: find the friction in how people work, vote, store, and decide — map the system — then automate and engineer it away. The flagship product is MILA, real-time social polling, live in public beta today. Alongside it, the company runs Lock & Go Storage LLC, builds production workflow automation for high-volume operations, and develops finished software assets like DRAE.

The company is independent and community-supported — no venture backers. It is supported by its products and its community; the free tiers of its sites carry clearly-labeled advertising so the tools can stay free to use — never by selling user data. Its papers are in order: formed in Delaware, operated from Troy, Michigan, with business contact at edward@milatech.tech.

02 / Our story

Started with one product. Built into a company.

Mila Tech LLC was formed in Delaware on August 3, 2026 (Delaware file number 10721454) and is operated from Troy, Michigan, in Metro Detroit. It was founded around a product already being built: MILA, real-time social polling meant to give everyday people a faster, friendlier public square. The work around it — backends, mobile clients, payments, moderation, relentless beta testing — became the company’s foundation.

Two more products followed. Owed grew out of a simple observation: money already owed to ordinary people — settlements and unclaimed property — goes unclaimed because the claiming process defeats them, so Owed finds it and prepares the claim. DRAE, a library governing ML ensembles and AI agents by expert disagreement, came from asking who watches intelligent systems when they drift. Edward also runs Lock & Go Storage LLC with the same systems mindset.

03 / How we work

Map the system. Automate it. Prove it works.

Every Mila Tech project runs the same loop. First, find the friction — the manual step, the confusing form, the decision nobody can make at the speed of now. Second, map the system honestly: who touches it, where it breaks, what it costs. Third, automate and engineer the friction out — a real-time backend, a browser automation that saves hours a week, or a governance rule that halts a drifting model. Fourth, commercialize what survives: if it cannot help a real person or a real business, it does not ship.

We also build in public, because trust is part of the product. Mila Tech publishes Daily Updates as work ships, and MILA’s beta runs against a public Fix Tracker: 447+ bug-hunt issues found, fixed, and re-tested under attack, posted for anyone to read. Testing includes forced-failure testing — deliberately breaking sign-ins, payments, and permissions to check the system fails safely — and our published counts are the real counts, even when small.

06 / How Mila Tech makes money

Honest revenue, labeled honestly

Mila Tech’s revenue model is deliberately plain. Products charge subscriptions at published prices: MILA Premium is $2.99 per month or $19.99 per year, with coin packs for flash polls, and Owed offers a $6.99-per-month subscription for hunting, filing preparation, and tracking. Enterprise offers sit on top — MILA for Brands, Owed Enterprise, DRAE pilots and licensing — and the Services line packages Edward’s expertise into scoped engagements.

We label the early stage honestly: DRAE is pre-revenue, several enterprise offers are in pilot stage rather than producing recurring revenue today, and community donations stand at $0 raised so far. Numbers on this site are numbers we can point to.

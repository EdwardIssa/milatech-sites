About

A builder-led technology company.

Mila Tech LLC is a Delaware limited liability company based in Metro Detroit, Michigan. We build consumer software, workflow automation, and intelligent systems — end to end, from cloud backend to physical hardware.

01 / The company

Find friction. Engineer it out.

Mila Tech’s loop is simple: find the friction in how people work, vote, store, and decide — map the system — then automate and engineer it away. The flagship product is MILA, real-time social polling, live in public beta today. Alongside it, the company runs Lock & Go Storage LLC, builds production workflow automation for high-volume operations, and develops finished software assets like DRAE.

The company is independent and community-supported — no venture backers, no ads-first roadmap. Its papers are in order: formed in Delaware, operated from Troy, Michigan, with business contact at edward@milatech.tech.

01 / The math

Stop renting your own leads

Per-result lead services rent you your own leads. LeadMiner X sits on the official API instead — one purchase, and your only ongoing cost is Google’s raw API rate on your key. The rate works per request, not per lead: each request returns up to 20 leads, and Google gives you the first 1,000 requests every month free.

The comparison

Metered service vs. owning the tool

Outscraper, the best-known per-result service, charges $3 per 1,000 records (first 500 a month free) — and that’s every run, forever. Re-run the same city next quarter for a fresh list and you pay for the same businesses again. The other route — scraping Google Maps directly — violates Google’s terms and breaks every time they change the page. (Using LeadMiner X doesn’t make every downstream use compliant by itself: you’re responsible for using exported data in line with Google’s terms and the laws that apply to your outreach, same as any list you buy or build.)

Program details

Gumroad runs the program

Tracking, attribution, and payouts are handled end to end by Gumroad under Gumroad's own terms — we do not pay commission outside Gumroad, so there is never a second set of numbers to reconcile. Read Gumroad's official explainer before you sell: Becoming an affiliate on Gumroad — Gumroad Help Center.

30-day cookie, last click wins

Because we add you as one of our own affiliates directly, your link sets a 30-day cookie: if someone clicks your link and buys within 30 days, you are credited. If they click a different affiliate's link after yours, the most recent click gets the credit — that is Gumroad's rule for everyone.

Only your affiliate link credits you

A plain product link earns you nothing — Gumroad can only credit sales made through your personal affiliate link (the one in your dashboard and in the email Gumroad sends you). Link shorteners like bit.ly are fine, as long as they point at your affiliate URL. If a customer's browser blocks cookies, Gumroad's sticky-link format (your affiliate ID in the product URL) still credits you — the Help Center article above shows how it works.

How and when Gumroad pays

Gumroad pays affiliates the same way it pays creators: to the payout settings on your Gumroad account, on Gumroad's payout schedule, once your cleared balance reaches Gumroad's $100 minimum. Keep your payout settings current — Gumroad cannot pay an account that has not filled them in.

Refunds reverse commission

LeadMiner X carries a 14-day money-back guarantee. If a customer is refunded, 100% of the sale price goes back to them — and the commission on that sale comes back too, including from your balance if you were already paid out. That is Gumroad's rule, and it is the honest cost of a real guarantee.

Play it straight

Disclose that your link is an affiliate link wherever you post it (the FTC requires it, and your audience deserves it). No spam, no fake reviews, no claims we do not make ourselves — the selling points on this page are the whole truth about this product, and they are enough.
